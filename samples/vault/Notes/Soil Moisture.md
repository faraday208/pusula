# Soil Moisture

How the probe readings map to watering decisions.

> [!note] Calibration
> Calibrate in dry air first, then in a glass of water. The two readings are your 0% and 100%.

| Reading | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| below 30% | dry | water now |
| 30% to 60% | good | wait |
| above 60% | wet | skip the next watering |

> [!warning] Probe corrosion
> Cheap resistive probes corrode within weeks. Use capacitive probes, as decided in [[Decisions]].

The weekly routine is in [[Watering Schedule]].
