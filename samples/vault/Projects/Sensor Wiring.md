# Sensor Wiring

How the probe and the temperature sensor connect to the board.

| Pin | Signal | Wire |
|---|---|---|
| 3V3 | Power | red |
| GND | Ground | black |
| A0 | Soil probe | yellow |
| D4 | Temperature | green |

Keep the probe cable shorter than 30 cm, or the readings get noisy.

Part of [[Greenhouse Sensor]].
