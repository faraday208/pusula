# Decisions

A short log, newest first.

## 2026-09-20: Capacitive probes

Chosen over resistive probes. They cost a little more, but resistive ones corrode ([[Soil Moisture]] has the details). This raises the [[Budget]] by about 8.

## 2026-09-12: Report every ten minutes

Often enough to see a dry spell coming, rarely enough for the battery to last a month.

## 2026-09-05: One board for sensing and radio

Keeps the wiring in [[Greenhouse Sensor]] simple and the enclosure small.
